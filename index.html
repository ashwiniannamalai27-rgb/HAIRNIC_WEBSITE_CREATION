How To Use Our Natural Shampoo

Follow these 3 easy steps for fuller, healthier hair.

1. Wet Hair Thoroughly

Rinse scalp and hair with lukewarm water to open follicles.

2. Massage Into Scalp

Apply a coin-sized amount and work into a rich lather for 2 mins.

3. Rinse Clean

Wash completely with cold or lukewarm water and towel dry gently.
