1. Information We Collect

When you browse our catalog, make an online purchase, or subscribe to our 30% newsletter discount, we collect personal contact details including your full name, email address, postal address, and phone number.

We use your details strictly to fulfill product deliveries, communicate tracking numbers, process refunds, and notify you regarding promotional discounts. We do not sell, rent, or lease your private information to third-party marketing companies.

All payment transactions are encrypted using Industry Standard Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) technology. We never store raw debit/credit card credentials on our servers.

If you have any questions or wish to delete your stored user data, contact our data administrator at privacy@hairnic.com.
